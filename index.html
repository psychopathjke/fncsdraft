What to do and how it works - attributes, rating, scoring, sources

What to do - four steps

1. Pick a mode, a region and the seasons20 seconds
2. Open packs and keep one card from eachthe rest go back
3. Add a weapon and a heal - both add squad power+1…+5
4. Run the tournament and see how far you getup to 12 games
The goal is a squad stronger than the lobby. The big number on a card is OVR, the player's overall power; the six attributes sit under it. Do not draft on the number alone - two players with the same OVR play very differently because their strengths differ. You get two pack rerolls per draft, and anyone already drafted will not appear again.

The six attributes - what they mean

AIM · Aimeliminations per match
END · Endgameshare of points from placement, not kills
SUR · Survivalaverage placement
CON · Consistencypoints per match
CLU · ClutchVictory Royales per match
EXP · Experiencetotal FNCS matches played
Each attribute is where the player ranked among the people who were in that lobby with them, not an abstract score. None of them contain a random number: hover an attribute on a card to see which column of the standings it was read from. High END with low AIM is a player who scores by surviving rather than fighting; the reverse is a fragger. The FRG or IGL role is printed under the OVR.

How FNCS Major mode works

Play-In - 2 days, points add uptop 150 advance
Heats - 3 heats of 5 games, winning a game = instant advanceotherwise top 15 by points
Failed Play-In or HeatsLast Chance Qualifier
Major Finals - 12 games, 45 from heats + 5 from LCQfinal placement
The same duo has to clear every stage in a row - fail a stage and the run stops, showing exactly where you were eliminated. Any number of teams can be in Play-In (real players plus reserve teams for scale), but reserve teams can never advance past Play-In - only real players reach Heats and the Grands.

Tournament scoring - same system as fortnitetracker.com

Victory Royale+9
Each threshold in top 2–5+4
Each threshold in top 6–25+2
Top 26 and below+0
Each elimination+4
This is a cumulative bracket system: your placement score is the sum of every threshold you cleared (e.g. 1st place = 9 for the win + 4×4 for top2–5 + 2×20 for top6–25 = 65 points). The resulting numbers (65/56/52…2/0) match the real FNCS Grand Finals placement table.

Where the rating comes from

The Summit LAN - Major 1 cards onlyweight 3.0
Heats · Play-In · Last Chance0.9 · 0.5 · 0.4
Each Division 1 Cup weekweight 0.15
Grand Final of their own regionweight 2.0
A rating is a whole season, not one tournament. Every result a player has is blended by weight, so a champion who scraped through the qualifiers rates below someone who held form all season. Seasons never mix: a Major 1 card is rated on S40 alone and a Major 2 card on S41 alone - different patches, different meta, and a result in one cannot touch the other. Inside a region the ladder is stretched so its best player lands on the ceiling: 96 for Europe and NA Central, 85 elsewhere, where the field is thinner.

Squad synergy

Drafted real teammates together+5
Shared nationality between players+2 / player
In reality FNCS 2026 is played in duos - that is the real format. Trio and Squad are fantasy: the players are real, but you build the roster yourself. Synergy is drawn as a triangle (trio) or square (squad), and the lines light up for real teammates or compatriots.

Card rarity:
The colour comes from the very OVR printed on the card - what you see is what you get. The ladder is the one Fortnite uses and the colours match the game.
• 95+ · Mythic (gold)
• 90–94 · Legendary (orange)
• 80–89 · Epic (purple)
• 70–79 · Rare (blue)
• 60–69 · Uncommon (green)
• below 60 · Common (grey)
Mythic is for a season with no weak stage in it - the Grand Final, the heats and the cups all held at once. The same six colours tint the weapons and heals in the loot packs.
What's confirmed: names and region come from official participant lists and final standings (Liquipedia, Esports Earnings, esports.gg, fortnitetracker.com, and screenshots you sent).

About lobby size: there aren't enough real players to exactly hit the target team counts - missing AI-lobby slots (except in the Grands) are filled with clearly labeled reserve teams, shown without a flag.

Flags: nationality isn't confirmed for everyone - where there's no source, no flag is shown.

A rating is built from every result a player had that season: on a Major 1 card the Summit LAN carries the most, then the regional Grand Final, Heats, Play-In, Last Chance and the weekly Division 1 Cups. Seasons never mix — Major 1 is rated on S40, Major 2 on S41. The six attributes come from the published standings too: AIM is average eliminations, SUR average placement, CLU Victory Royales per match, CON points per match, END the share of points earned by placement rather than kills, and EXP the total FNCS matches played. Each is a rank among the people who were in that same lobby; none contain a random number — hover one to see its source. Only the tournament itself is simulated after the draft.
