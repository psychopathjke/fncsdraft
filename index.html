How FNCS Major mode works

Play-In — 2 days, points add uptop 150 advance
Heats — 6 sessions, winning a game = instant advanceotherwise top 15 by points
Failed Play-In or HeatsLast Chance Qualifier
Major Finals — 12 games, 45 from heats + 5 from LCQfinal placement
The same duo has to clear every stage in a row — fail a stage and the run stops, showing exactly where you were eliminated. Any number of teams can be in Play-In (real players plus reserve teams for scale), but reserve teams can never advance past Play-In — only real players reach Heats and the Grands.

Tournament scoring — same system as fortnitetracker.com

Victory Royale+9
Each threshold in top 2–5+4
Each threshold in top 6–25+2
Top 26 and below+0
Each elimination+4
This is a cumulative bracket system: your placement score is the sum of every threshold you cleared (e.g. 1st place = 9 for the win + 4×4 for top2–5 + 2×20 for top6–25 = 65 points). The resulting numbers (65/56/52…2/0) match the real FNCS Grand Finals placement table.

In reality FNCS 2026 is played in duos (2 players) — that is now the real format in the game. Trio and Squad are fantasy modes: the players are real, but you build the roster yourself, like in 322-0. Synergy is drawn as a triangle (trio) or square (squad) — lines between players light up if they are real teammates or compatriots.

How rating and rarity are worked out:
• 100 · Mythic (gold) — won an actual offline LAN (Global Championship, Invitational 2022, Major 1 Summit)
• 90 · Legendary (orange) — won any online tournament (seasonal Grand Finals, Major, All-Star Showdown)
• 80–89 · Epic (purple) — top-10 finish
• 70–79 · Rare (blue) — any finish below top 10
• 60–69 · Uncommon (green) and 50–59 · Common (gray) — appeared in the event with no published placement
Minimum card rating is 50.
What's confirmed: names and region come from official participant lists and final standings (Liquipedia, Esports Earnings, esports.gg, fortnitetracker.com, and screenshots you sent).

About lobby size: there aren't enough real players to exactly hit the target team counts — missing AI-lobby slots (except in the Grands) are filled with clearly labeled reserve teams, shown without a flag.

Flags: nationality isn't confirmed for everyone — where there's no source, no flag is shown.
