Your career

You get one player and the real calendar. Cups during the week, Majors when Epic ran them, then Reload, the LANs and the Nations Cup. A good week moves you up a division, a bad one sends you down.

  • 2019 to 2026. Pick a year and play it in the team format it really had. 2024 had no divisions, so your 2024 doesn't either.
  • The big stages. Open qualifiers, heats, last chance and the grand final, with Epic's rules for each season. Then Düsseldorf, Antwerp and the Esports World Cup.
  • Between nights. Duo partners, clubs and contracts, a coach, form and energy, and a feed where the scene talks about you.

With friends

Play a duo career together: same night, same lobby, same standings on both screens. Or start a race with up to five friends and see whose career goes further.

Run a club

In Fortnite Manager you are the one paying the players. Sign a roster, watch the budget, train the team and take it through an FNCS season.

Draft

Build a squad from real FNCS cards and play the tournament they were in: play-in, heats, last chance, grand final, the LAN. You also choose where to land. Hot drops pay more if you survive them.

Where the ratings come from

A card is one player's season in one number, worked out from results. Bigger stages weigh more, so a grand final counts for more than a heat.

About 87,000 cards from more than 600 tournaments, from the 2019 World Cup to this season, in Europe, NA Central, NA West, Brazil, Asia, the Middle East and Oceania.

Questions

Does Fortnite have a career mode?

Not in the game itself. FNCS Draft is a free fan-made one: you play a competitive career season after season on the real FNCS calendar.

Is it free?

Yes. No account, nothing to install, nothing to pay. Your careers are saved in your browser.

Are the players real?

Yes. Every card is a real FNCS player, and the rating comes from what they actually did that season.

Can I play with a friend?

Yes. Play a duo career together on the same tournament nights, or race up to five friends to see whose career goes further.

Can I continue on another device?

Yes. Turn on Cloud on the careers screen, you get a 16-character code. Type it on the other phone or computer and your careers come with you. Keep the code to yourself, it works like a password.

Which languages?

English, Russian, French, Italian, Portuguese, German and Spanish.

Is this made by Epic Games?

No. It is an independent fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Epic Games. Fortnite and FNCS are trademarks of Epic Games.
