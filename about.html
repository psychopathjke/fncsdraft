FNCS Draft is a free fan-made Fortnite career mode and FNCS draft simulator. It runs in the browser with no install and no signup. All the players are real FNCS players, and their ratings come from their tournament results.

Career mode

You start as a rookie and play one season after another on the real FNCS calendar. The other players in your lobbies are the people who played FNCS that year.

  • Years. Every year from 2019 to 2026, each with the team format it actually used.
  • Divisions. Good weeks in the cups move you up, bad ones move you down. 2024 had no divisions, so that year doesn't have them either.
  • Majors. Open qualifiers, heats, last chance and the grand final, following Epic's rules for each season.
  • Reload Championship and the LANs: Düsseldorf, Antwerp and the Esports World Cup.
  • Nations Cup and the solo events, with national teams made of each country's best players.
  • Off the server. Duo and trio partners, clubs and contracts, managers, coaches, energy and form, and a social feed.

Play the career with a friend

Two people can play the same tournament night together. Both browsers run the same simulation and only send each other their choices, so both see the same standings.

Fortnite Manager

Run a club instead of a player: sign players, set the budget, train the team and take it through the FNCS season.

The real scene

The home page follows the real FNCS: a countdown to the next Epic event, the upcoming FNCS stages and cups with their prize pools, this year's prize money by player with flags and medals, and the site's own leaderboard of careers.

Draft mode

Pick a team of real FNCS players and play through the tournament they were in: play-in, heats, last chance, grand finals and the LAN. You choose where to land. Contested spots are worth more but riskier.

Where the cards come from

Ratings are calculated from results. A card uses the player's whole season, and bigger stages count for more, so a grand final matters more than a heat.

There are about 87 000 cards from more than 600 tournaments, from the 2019 World Cup to the current season, in Europe, NA Central, NA West, Brazil, Asia, the Middle East and Oceania.

FAQ

Does Fortnite have a career mode?

Not in the game. FNCS Draft is a fan-made one based on the competitive calendar.

Does it cost anything?

No. There's no signup and no account. Your career is saved in your browser.

Can I play on another device?

Yes. Turn on Cloud on the careers screen and you get a 16-character code. Enter it on another phone or computer and your careers and manager come with it. Keep the code private: it works like a password.

What languages is it in?

English, Russian, French, Italian, Portuguese, German and Spanish.

Is this made by Epic Games?

No. It's an independent fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Epic Games. Fortnite and FNCS are trademarks of Epic Games.
