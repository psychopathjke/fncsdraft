'''Fortnite Champion Series: Chapter 2 Season 2 - Heat 3: Europe''' is the third heat of qualifiers hosted in Europe for the '''Fortnite Champion Series: Chapter 2 Season 2 - Grand Finals'''. Each of the four Europe group heats features a prize pool of $68,400 {{Abbr/USD}}.

==About==

===Format===
*'''Heat 3:'''  ''April 18th, 2020''
** 3 hour limit & 6 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 12 duos will advance to Grand Finals.
