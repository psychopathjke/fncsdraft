'''Fortnite Champion Series: Chapter 2 Season 3 - Qualifier 4: NA East''' is the fourth qualifier hosted in North America for the '''Fortnite Champion Series: Chapter 2 Season 3 - Grand Finals'''. Each week of the NA East qualifiers features a prize pool of $31,250 {{Abbr/USD}}.

===Format===
*'''Round 1:'''  ''August 9th, 2020''
** 7 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 3,000 players will advance to Round 2.
*'''Round 2:'''  ''August 9th, 2020''
** 7 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 100 players will advance to Round 3.
*'''Round 3:''' ''August 9th, 2020''
** 6 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 50 players will advance to Season Finals.
*** If a player has already qualified, their spot will be transferred to the player with the most cumulative points across all four qualifiers.
*** Top 200 players that didn't qualify will be invited to fill out rest of the Season Finals spots.
