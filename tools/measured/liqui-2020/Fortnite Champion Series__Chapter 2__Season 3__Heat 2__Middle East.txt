'''Fortnite Champion Series: Chapter 2 Season 3 - Heat 2: Middle East''' is the second heat of qualifiers hosted in Middle East for the '''Fortnite Champion Series: Chapter 2 Season 3 - Grand Finals'''. Each of the Middle East group heats features a prize pool of $10,000 {{Abbr/USD}}.

==About==

===Format===
*'''Season Finals Heat:'''  ''August 14th, 2020''
** 6 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 50 players will advance to Grand Finals.
