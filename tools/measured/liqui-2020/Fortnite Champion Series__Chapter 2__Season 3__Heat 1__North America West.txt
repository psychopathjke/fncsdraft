'''Fortnite Champion Series: Chapter 2 Season 3 - Heat 2: NA West''' is the first heat of qualifiers hosted in North America for the '''Fortnite Champion Series: Chapter 2 Season 3 - Grand Finals'''. Each of the four NA West group heats features a prize pool of $20,000 {{Abbr/USD}}.

==About==

===Format===
*'''Season Finals Heat:'''  ''August 14th, 2020''
** 6 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 25 players will advance to Grand Finals.
