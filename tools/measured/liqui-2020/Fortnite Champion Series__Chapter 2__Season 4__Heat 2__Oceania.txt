'''Fortnite Champion Series: Chapter 2 Season 4 - Heat 2: Oceania''' is the second heat of qualifiers hosted in Oceania for the '''Fortnite Champion Series: Chapter 2 Season 4 - Grand Finals'''. Each of the Oceania group heats features a prize pool of $0 {{Abbr/USD}}.

==About==
===Format===
*'''Heat 2 (Day 1):''' ''October 29th, 2020''
** 6 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
*'''Heat 2 (Day 2):''' ''October 30th, 2020''
** 6 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 16 teams after both days of the heat will advance to Season Finals.
** Placements 17-32 after both days of the heat will advance to the Wildcard Match.
