'''Fortnite Champion Series: Chapter 2 Season 2 - Week 2: NA East''' is the second week of qualifiers hosted in North America East for the '''Fortnite Champion Series: Chapter 2 Season 2 - Grand Finals'''. Each week of the NA East qualifiers features a prize pool of $31,250 {{Abbr/USD}}.

==About==

===Format===
*'''Open:'''  ''March 28th, 2020''
** 3 hour limit & 10 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 1,000 duos will advance to Semifinals.
*'''Semifinals:'''  ''March 28th, 2020''
** 3 hour limit & 10 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Points scored during Open are not transferred to Semifinals.
** Top 50 duos will advance to Finals.
*'''Finals:''' ''March 29th, 2020''
** 6 match limit
** Points scored during Semifinals are not transferred to Finals.
** Top 20 duos will advance to Season Finals.
*** If a duo has already qualified, their spot will be transferred to the closest placing duo that has not qualified.
*** Top 120 duos that didn't qualify will be invited to fill out rest of the Season Finals spots.
