'''Fortnite Champion Series: Chapter 2 Season 4 - Week 1: NA East''' is the first week of qualifiers hosted in North America for the '''Fortnite Champion Series: Chapter 2 Season 4 - Grand Finals'''. Each week of the NA East qualifiers features a prize pool of $36,855 {{Abbr/USD}}.

===Format===
*'''Round 1:'''  ''October 9th, 2020''
** 3 hour limit & 10 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 1,000 teams will advance to Round 2.
*'''Round 2:'''  ''October 10th, 2020''
** 3 hour limit & 10 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 33 teams will advance to Round 3.
*'''Round 3 (Day 1):''' ''October 10th, 2020''
** 6 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
*'''Round 3 (Day 2):''' ''October 11th, 2020''
** 6 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 15 teams after both days of Round 3 will advance to Season Finals.
*** Top 87 teams that didn't through a top 15 placement during any week will be invited to fill out the remaining Season Finals spots.
*** If a team has already qualifies multiple times, their spot will be transferred to the team with the most cumulative points across all three weeks.
