'''Fortnite Champion Series: Chapter 2 Season 4 - Week 3: Oceania''' is the third week of qualifiers hosted in Oceania for the '''Fortnite Champion Series: Chapter 2 Season 4 - Grand Finals'''. Each week of the Oceania qualifiers features a prize pool of $9,450 {{Abbr/USD}}.

==About==
===Format===
*'''Round 1:'''  ''October 23rd, 2020''
** 3 hour limit & 10 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 350 teams will advance to Round 2.
*'''Round 2:'''  ''October 24th, 2020''
** 3 hour limit & 10 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 33 teams will advance to Round 3.
*'''Round 3 (Day 1):''' ''October 24th, 2020''
** 6 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
*'''Round 3 (Day 2):''' ''October 25th, 2020''
** 6 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 8 teams after both days of Round 3 will advance to Season Finals.
*** Top 42 teams that didn't through a top 8 placement during any week will be invited to fill out the remaining Season Finals spots.
*** If a team has already qualifies multiple times, their spot will be transferred to the team with the most cumulative points across all three weeks.
