===Format===
* Players gain points in a series of six matches
* Players will be ranked on the leaderboards based on the scoring system listed below

{| class="wikitable"
|-
! Condition !! Points
|-
| Victory || 10
|-
| 2nd to 5th || 7
|-
| 6th to 15th || 5
|-
| 16th to 25th || 3
|-
| Each Elimination || 1
|}

Placement points are not cumulative across the same match. Tiebreakers will be used in the following order:
* Total Victory Royales
* Average Eliminations in the session
* Average Placement per match in the session
* Coin Flip
