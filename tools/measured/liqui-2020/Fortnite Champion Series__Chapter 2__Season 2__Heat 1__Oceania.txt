'''Fortnite Champion Series: Chapter 2 Season 2 - Heat 1: Oceania''' is the first heat of qualifiers hosted in Oceania for the '''Fortnite Champion Series: Chapter 2 Season 2 - Grand Finals'''. Each of the Oceania group heats features a prize pool of $12,500 {{Abbr/USD}}.

==About==

===Format===
*'''Heat 1:'''  ''April 17th, 2020''
** 3 hour limit & 6 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 25 duos will advance to Grand Finals.
