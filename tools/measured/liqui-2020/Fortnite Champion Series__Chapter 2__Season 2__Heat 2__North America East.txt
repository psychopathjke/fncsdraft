'''Fortnite Champion Series: Chapter 2 Season 2 - Heat 2: NA East''' is the second heat of qualifiers hosted in North America for the '''Fortnite Champion Series: Chapter 2 Season 2 - Grand Finals'''. Each of the four North America East group heats features a prize pool of $42,560 {{Abbr/USD}}.

==About==

===Format===
*'''Heat 2:'''  ''April 17th, 2020''
** 3 hour limit & 6 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 12 duos will advance to Grand Finals.
