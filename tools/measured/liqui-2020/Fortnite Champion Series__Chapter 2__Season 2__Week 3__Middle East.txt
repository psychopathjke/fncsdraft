'''Fortnite Champion Series: Chapter 2 Season 2 - Week 3: Middle East''' is the third week of qualifiers hosted in Middle East for the '''Fortnite Champion Series: Chapter 2 Season 2 - Grand Finals'''. Each week of the Middle East qualifiers features a prize pool of $6,250 {{Abbr/USD}}.

==About==

===Format===
*'''Open:'''  ''April 3rd, 2020''
** 3 hour limit & 10 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 400 duos will advance to Semifinals.
*'''Semifinals:'''  ''April 3rd, 2020''
** 3 hour limit & 10 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Points scored during Open are not transferred to Semifinals.
** Top 50 duos will advance to Finals.
*'''Finals:''' ''April 4th, 2020''
** 6 match limit
** Points scored during Semifinals are not transferred to Finals.
** Top 10 duos will advance to Season Finals.
*** If a duo has already qualified, their spot will be transferred to the closest placing duo that has not qualified.
*** Top 60 duos that didn't qualify will be invited to fill out rest of the Season Finals spots.
