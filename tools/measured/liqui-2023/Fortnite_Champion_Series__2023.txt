'''FNCS 2023''' is the seventeenth season of the '''Fortnite Champion Series'''. This season introduces the most massive changes to the FNCS system yet with an updated format. 3 splits - Major 1, Major 2, Major 3 - each having 3 weekly events leading to a major tournament. At its major tournaments, the best players from each region qualify for an international tournament that will determine this year's world champions.

===Format===
*'''Upper Bracket''' - ''October 13<sup>th</sup>, 2023'':
**50 duos qualified from Majors participate in this tournament.
**5 matches.
**Scoring based on placement, eliminations and mid-match objectives.
** {{bgcolortext|up|Top 25 Duos}} advance to Grand Finals.
** {{bgcolortext|stay|Bottom 25 Duos}} fall to Lower Bracket.
*'''Lower Bracket''' - ''October 14<sup>th</sup>, 2023'':
**25 duos from Upper Bracket and 24 qualified from Last Chance Major participate in this tournament.
**5 matches.
**Scoring based on placement, eliminations and mid-match objectives.
** {{bgcolortext|up|Top 25 Duos}} advance to Grand Finals.
** {{bgcolortext|down|Bottom 25 Duos}} are Eliminated.
*'''Grand Finals''' - ''October 15<sup>th</sup>, 2023'':
**25 duos from Upper Bracket and 25 from Lower Bracket participate in this tournament.
**6 matches.
**Scoring based on placement, eliminations and mid-match objectives.
** {{bgcolortext|byeup|Winning Duo}} is the new '''Global Champion'''.
