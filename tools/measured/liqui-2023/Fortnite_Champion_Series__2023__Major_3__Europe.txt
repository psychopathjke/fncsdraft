'''FNCS 2023''' is the seventeenth season of the '''Fortnite Champion Series'''. This season introduces the most massive changes to the FNCS system yet with an updated format. 3 splits - Major 1, Major 2, Major 3 - each having 3 weekly events leading to a major tournament. At its major tournaments, the best players from each region qualify for an international tournament that will determine this year's world champions.

===Format===
*'''Grand Finals''': ''August 12<sup>th</sup> - 13<sup>th</sup>, 2023 ''
**12 games spread over two days.
***6 games every day.
**Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
{{FNCS 2023 points format 2}}
** {{bgcolortext|up|Top 10 Duos}} advance to [[Fortnite_Champion_Series/2023|Global Championship Upper Bracket]].
*** If a duo has already qualified, their spot will be transferred to the closest placing duo that has not qualified.
