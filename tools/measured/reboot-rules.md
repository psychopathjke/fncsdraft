# Reboot Vans / Cards in competitive Fortnite, 2019-2026 (researched 29.10.2026)

## Main finding
I found NO source (Epic patch notes, FNCS official rules, fandom wiki, Liquipedia, press) for a
"Reboot Vans disabled after zone X" rule in competitive. FNCS official rules (2025) contain
no game-settings section at all (format/eligibility/conduct/scoring only). Wiki pages for Arena,
Ranked, FNCS and Late Game Arena all just say "Reboot Vans are enabled (unusable in Solos)".
=> zone cutoff: UNKNOWN; most likely none (vans usable all game, limited only by card timer,
   van cooldown and the van being in zone). Do not invent a cutoff.

## Timeline
- v8.30 (10.04.2019): Reboot Vans added. Disabled in Solos, big-team LTMs, respawn modes.
- ~19.04.2019: Epic disabled vans in ALL competitive modes (scoring bug: kills/score with
  rebooted teammates), day before 20.04 World Cup Duos qualifier.
- ~04.05.2019: back in Arena and tournaments (World Cup Online Open week 4). Exact patch UNKNOWN.
- Ch2S4 v14.40-v14.50 (Fortnitemares 2020): vans disabled in core BR; comp UNKNOWN.
- Since then: always on in team comp (Arena, Ranked, FNCS trios/duos, cash cups). Solo comp: no vans
  (since Ch7S1 vans not even spawned in Solo).
- Reload playlists: no vans/cards; timed respawns until a late-match cutoff (Reload only, confirmed;
  no other comp mode in Ch5-Ch7 uses Reload respawns, as far as found).
