===Format===
*'''Round 1:'''  ''May 2, 2022''
** 3 hour limit & 10 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 2000 duos will advance to Round 2.

*'''Round 2:'''  ''May 4, 2022''
** 3 hour limit & 10 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 500 duos will advance to Round 3.

*'''Round 3:'''  ''May 5, 2022''
** 3 hour limit & 10 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 50 duos will advance to Round 4.

*'''Round 4:'''  ''May 6, 2022''
** 3 hour limit & 6 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 5 duos will advance to the [[Fortnite_Champion_Series/Chapter_3/Season_2/Grand Finals/Europe|Grand Finals]].
