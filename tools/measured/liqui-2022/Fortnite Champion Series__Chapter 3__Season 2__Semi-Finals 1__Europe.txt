===Format===
*'''Session 1:'''  ''May 20, 2022''
** 2.5 hour limit & 5 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Team qualification was defined by their '''[https://www.epicgames.com/fortnite/competitive/en-US/events/championseries-c3s2/apiStandings/series-leaderboards?r=2 Series Points]''' awarded in Qualifiers '''[[Fortnite_Champion_Series/Chapter_3/Season_2/Qualifier_1/Europe|1]]''', '''[[Fortnite_Champion_Series/Chapter_3/Season_2/Qualifier_1/Europe|2]]''', and '''[[Fortnite_Champion_Series/Chapter_3/Season_2/Qualifier_1/Europe|3]]''' during ''Rounds 3 and 4''
** Top 11 duos will advance to the [[Fortnite_Champion_Series/Chapter_3/Season_2/Grand Finals/Europe|Grand Finals]].
***The 5 duos who got a victory royale during the session
***The 6 best duos according to the end of the session ranking
**The remaining duos will have another chance to qualify for the [[Fortnite_Champion_Series/Chapter_3/Season_2/Grand Finals/Europe|Grand Finals]] in the [[Fortnite_Champion_Series/Chapter_3/Season_2/Semi-Finals_2/Europe|Semi-Finals 2]].
