'''FNCS Chapter 3 - Season 3''' is the third Fortnite Champions Series of this year 2022. Players have 3 qualifiers to qualify for the '''[[Fortnite_Champion_Series/Chapter_3/Season_3/Grand_Finals/Europe|Grand Finals]]''', '''qualifiers''' [[Fortnite_Champion_Series/Chapter_3/Season_3/Qualifier_1/Europe|1]], [[Fortnite Champion Series/Chapter 3/Season 3/Qualifier 2/Europe|2]] and [[Fortnite Champion Series/Chapter 3/Season 3/Qualifier 3/Europe|3]]. Duos score [https://www.epicgames.com/fortnite/competitive/en-US/events/championseries-c3s3/apiStandings/series-leaderboards?r=2 '''series points'''] during these qualifiers. The most consistent who failed to qualify in these qualifiers will have other chances to qualify during '''semi-finals''' [[Fortnite_Champion_Series/Chapter_3/Season_3/Semi-Finals_1/Europe|1]], [[Fortnite Champion Series/Chapter 3/Season 3/Semi-Finals 2/Europe|2]] and [[Fortnite Champion Series/Chapter 3/Season 3/Semi-Finals 3/Europe|3]]. At the end of all these chances, the top 50 duos from each region will fight in order to clinch the FNCS Championship title.

===Format===  
*'''Grand Finals - Session 1''': ''August 13th, 2022 ''
**3 hour limit & 6 match limit 
**Scoring based on placement and eliminations. 

*'''Grand Finals - Session 2''': ''August 14th, 2022 ''
**3 hour limit & 6 match limit 
**Scoring based on placement and eliminations.

*'''How to win?''' 
**There are two ways to win:
***By scoring the most points during the 12 games.
***Be the first to have 3 Victory Royales and 475 points.
