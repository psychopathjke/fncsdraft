===Format===
*'''Round 1:'''  ''February 17, 2022''
** 3 hour limit & 10 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 250 duos will advance to Round 2.

*'''Round 2:'''  ''February 18, 2022''
** 2 hour limit & 7 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 50 duos will advance to Round 3.

*'''Round 3:'''  ''February 18, 2022''
** 3 hour limit & 6 match limit
** Scoring based on placement and eliminations.
** Top 8 duos will advance to the [[Fortnite_Champion_Series/Chapter_3/Season_1/Grand Finals/Europe|Grand Finals]].
